## Core Method

Style is treated as a match between inner message, body proportions, context and clothes. The service should not only make a look beautiful, but also answer:

- What impression should the person make?
- Which archetypal code supports this impression?
- What occasion and social context is the look for?
- Which seasonal trend is relevant, and how softly should it be integrated?
- Which accessory gives the look a finished point?

## Archetype Layer

### Warrior / Leader

Message: competence, strength, boundaries, confidence, action.

Use:

- straight and vertical lines;
- symmetry;
- structured shoulders;
- double-breasted closures;
- belts, buckles, zippers, pockets;
- leather, suede, metal, dense knit;
- black, red, protective shades, metallic accents.

Avoid for soft/romantic requests:

- too much aggression;
- heavy boots with every look;
- excessive logos and hard contrast.

## SS26 Trend Layer

Use trends as a spice, not as a costume.

Relevant directions from `Тренды SS26 .pdf`:

- bright colors;
- tropical and graphic prints;
- new femininity;
- Marie Antoinette / boudoir references, adapted softly;
- layering;
- movement in clothes;
- sculptural silhouettes;
- emphasized waist;
- low rise only when appropriate;
- transparency and lace in measured doses;
- metallic accents;
- pajama styling;
- denim;
- texture;
- asymmetry;
- accent accessories;
- play with proportions.

Trend integration principles:

- A trend can be a thing, cut, material, color, detail, print or styling trick.
- Trends never return in a pure historical copy; the service adapts the decade reference to the client's occasion.
- One outfit should normally use one clear trend idea plus one supporting accessory, not every trend at once.
- Rotate trend ideas across several looks so the user does not receive three versions of the same skirt, same bottom type or same visual formula.

Decade references:

- 60s: mini skirts, short jackets, straight dresses, graphic/geometric prints, cat-eye or graphic accessories.
- 70s: denim, suede, velvet, knitwear, midi length, wide trousers, boho movement, belts, yellow-green-brown palette.
- 80s: power dressing, strong lines, office influence, metallic, mini, accent accessories and sport-chic details.
- 90s: minimalism, white shirt, tank top, straight jeans, clean dress, denim, easy layering.
- 2000s: low rise when appropriate, fitted tops, mini skirts, asymmetry, glossy/metallic accents, plastic-like accessories.

Occasion trend routing:

- Office and meetings: use power dressing, 90s minimalism and new femininity softly; avoid minis, corsets, transparency and beach shoes.
- Casual: use denim, shorts, mini skirts, cotton shirts, tanks, soft bags and comfortable shoes; rotate between denim, short bottom and relaxed tactile formulas.
- Evening: use new femininity, boudoir elements, waist emphasis, asymmetry, lace/soft shine and expressive accessories.

## Accessory Layer

Accessories must be selected by:

- occasion;
- desired impression;
- line, shape and scale of the look;
- face and body scale;
- color logic.

Rules:

- Use one main accent when the outfit already has texture, print or unusual cut.
- A scarf can be styled on the head, neck, bag, waist or as a belt.
- Bag and shoes can match, repeat a color from the outfit, or work through a color scheme.
- Jewelry scale should match face scale and fabric density.
- Belts, brooches, scarves, ties, gloves and socks are style tricks for making a basic outfit personal.

## Product Selection Logic

The service must pick real product cards first, then build the outfit. It must not invent an item and attach a random product link.

For each outfit:

- use concrete product URL;
- store article/SKU;
- store color;
- store product image reference when available;
- keep budget limit;
- prefer several brands in one look when suitable;
- explain which rule from the style method was used.

## Occasion Rules

The occasion is a hard styling constraint, not a soft preference.

Vacation:

- summer, light, breathable, easy to walk in;
- avoid wool, down, fur, heavy suede, boots, strict leather bottoms and dense office layers;
- prefer tops, shirts, light skirts, sandals, ballet flats, scarves, light bags and fresh colors.

Office and meetings:

- business-appropriate, clean lines, controlled accent;
- avoid bandeau, corset, minis, leggings, heavy evening styling, beach shoes and overly romantic exposure;
- prefer shirts, straight trousers, midi skirts, structured bags, ballet flats or closed shoes, small jewelry.

Evening:

- feminine, romantic, tactile, with a clear accent near face or waist;
- prefer corset-like tops, skirts, wrap/asymmetry, mules, ballet flats, earrings, scarves, small/expressive bags;
- avoid windbreakers, puffers, caps, overly casual or office-flat formulas.
